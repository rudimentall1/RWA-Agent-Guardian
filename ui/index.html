The payer accepts a synthetic invoice, funds escrow, and authorizes a narrowly capped executor. The optional Ollama runner proposes ALLOW, WAIT, or BLOCK from on-chain facts; strict policy checks cap its amount before execution. In AI mode, an EIP-712 signed intent binds the decision to the context and is verified by the upgraded executor, while the settlement contract enforces the mandate. The invoice is synthetic and the model does not verify legal truth.
